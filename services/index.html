Which plumbing service do you need?

Plumbing problems fall into a few families, and each has its own page with a clear checklist of what to do first. Start with the family that matches what you see, then follow the link for the detail.

If you are not sure, call and describe it. The dispatcher will point you to the right plumber, and the line is answered 24/7 for anything urgent.

First, safety

If it is an emergency, do this before browsing

  1. Water flooding: shut the main valve, then call.
  2. Sewage backing up: stop using water, do not flush, then call.
  3. Gas smell: leave the building, then call 911 and Enbridge Gas at 1-800-767-1689 first. Call us after the gas company clears the home.

None of these steps needs a plumber on site, and each stops damage while you wait for one.

How we pick

Emergency or scheduled: how we sort your call

An emergency is anything that is damaging property, putting people at risk or leaving a home without water, heat or a working toilet. A slow drain or a dripping faucet is not an emergency, and it still gets fast, honest service.

Scheduled work, such as installing a fixture or a repipe, starts with a conversation about timing and a written scope. We do not use an emergency visit to sell work you do not need.

What to expect

What every visit includes

  • A plumber who finds the cause before suggesting a fix.
  • A clear price before work starts, and a call before the scope changes.
  • Licensed and insured work, and a name you can check with the state.
  • A tested repair and a plain explanation of what to watch for.

The Utah Division of Occupational and Professional Licensing runs a free Verify a License tool, and it is worth using on any plumber you hire.
