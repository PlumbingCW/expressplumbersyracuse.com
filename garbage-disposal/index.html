Safety first

Disposal jammed or leaking: what to do now

A jammed disposal is common, and a leak under the sink needs attention. Emergency garbage disposal repair in Syracuse, UT is answered 24/7. Keep your hand out of the unit and follow these steps while you wait.

  1. Switch the disposal off at the wall and at the breaker.
  2. Never reach in with a hand. Use tongs to remove anything you can see.
  3. Many units have a hex socket under the base. A hex key turns the plate and frees a jam.
  4. If the unit leaks, shut the valve under the sink and put a bowl below it.

What not to grind

What breaks a disposal

  • Grease and fat, which coat the pipe and the blades.
  • Fibrous food such as celery, corn husks and artichoke.
  • Coffee grounds in bulk, which pack into a sludge.
  • Bones, fruit pits and hard shells.
  • Non-food items such as utensils and bottle caps.

Run cold water while the unit runs and for a few seconds after. Cold water keeps grease solid so it moves through, and warm water melts it onto the pipe wall.

Local note

Why grease matters to the whole sewer system

The North Davis Sewer District treats the wastewater of Syracuse and its neighbors at a plant in Syracuse, and the District lists grease among the things that do not belong in the system. A disposal sends more food waste into your drain, and grease from it can build up in the line.

If your sink drain is slow even with the disposal clear, see drain cleaning, and for a drain that backs up repeatedly look at hydro jetting.

Repair or replace

Deciding between a repair and a new unit

A jam, a tripped reset or a loose connection is a repair. A leak from the housing, a seized motor or a unit that has run for many years is a replacement. We install a unit you choose or recommend a horsepower to match your use.

Replacement includes the mounting, the drain connection and the electrical connection. If the dishwasher drain ties into the unit, we reconnect it and test for leaks.

Care

Habits that keep a disposal running

Run cold water before, during and for fifteen seconds after each use. Feed small amounts at a time. Freeze citrus peel in an ice tray and grind it now and then to scrape the walls. Avoid harsh chemical cleaners, which damage seals and make later repairs risky for the plumber.

Code note

Air gaps and the dishwasher connection

Utah's state construction code amends the International Plumbing Code (IPC) for use statewide, under Utah Code Title 15A, Chapter 3, Part 3. A dishwasher drain tied to a disposal needs an air gap or a high loop to keep dirty water from flowing back.

If your dishwasher drain sags below the disposal inlet, tell the plumber. It is a common cause of smells and backflow into the dishwasher.
