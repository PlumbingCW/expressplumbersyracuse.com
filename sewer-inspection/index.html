What it is

Sewer camera inspection in Syracuse, UT

A sewer camera inspection puts a small waterproof camera on a flexible cable and runs it through your drain line while a monitor shows the pipe in real time. It finds roots, cracks, offsets, sags and buildup, and it shows where the damage sits so a repair can be scoped without guesswork.

This page is for planned inspections. If sewage is backing up now, the line is answered 24/7 on the sewer line cleaning page, and we bring the camera after the line is clear.

What it shows

What the camera can and cannot tell you

  • Roots entering joints, and how heavy the growth is.
  • Cracks, offsets and collapsed sections.
  • A sag or belly where water sits and solids collect.
  • Grease, scale and debris on the pipe wall.
  • A foreign object that a cable could not clear.

A camera does not show the condition of every pipe in the house. It covers the line it travels through, so we pick the entry point to see the stretch that matters most.

Local note

Newer subdivisions, older lines nearby

Syracuse has grown fast since 1990, and most homes use newer pipe. That is good news for roots and corrosion, though newer lines can still have bellies from settling soil or poor bedding. The camera answers that for your lot.

Sewer service across the city runs through the North Davis Sewer District, whose plant is in Syracuse near the Great Salt Lake shoreline. If footage ever shows a problem beyond your property line, the District and Syracuse City Public Works are the people to contact.
