Shared infrastructure

Who handles sewers and water around here

The North Davis Sewer District serves Clearfield, Clinton, Layton, Roy, Sunset, Syracuse and West Point, and a small part of Kaysville. If sewage is backing up in your home, the blockage is usually in your own pipes. If neighbors have the same trouble, tell the District too. Its daytime line is 801.825.0712.

City public works departments handle water meters and city mains. Syracuse City Public Works is at 801.825.7235, and Layton City Public Works is at 801.336.3720. Call the city for anything on the city side of the meter, and call us for the pipes on your side.

Right now

Plumbing emergency anywhere in this area

  1. Shut off the main water valve for any flooding.
  2. Stop using water and do not flush for a sewage backup.
  3. For a gas smell, leave the building, then call 911 and Enbridge Gas at 1-800-767-1689 first.

Then call the 24/7 line. We tell you what else to do while a plumber is on the way.

What helps us dispatch faster

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • The kind of problem: water, sewer, gas smell or no hot water.
  • Whether you have shut the main water valve already.
  • A good callback number and whether someone will be home.
  • Gate codes or pets the plumber should know about.

Give us the details at the start of the call. The dispatcher confirms coverage, tells you what to do while you wait and passes your notes to the plumber.

Planning ahead

Things worth doing before an emergency happens

  • Find your main water shutoff and make sure everyone in the house knows where it is.
  • Save the dispatch number, the gas utility number and the District's number in your phone.
  • Test your sump pump and your water heater's relief valve each year.
  • Keep a flashlight and a small adjustable wrench near the main.

Five minutes of preparation turns a flooded floor into a closed valve and a phone call. The same steps apply in every town we cover.
