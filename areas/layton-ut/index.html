Layton emergencies

Plumbing emergency in Layton: do this first

An emergency plumber in Layton, UT is one call away, any hour. The line is answered 24/7, and a plumber leaves from Syracuse. Before we arrive, these moves limit the damage.

  1. Burst or leaking pipe: shut the main water valve and switch off the water heater.
  2. Sewer backup: stop using water, do not flush and keep everyone out of the affected room.
  3. Gas smell: leave the building and call 911 and Enbridge Gas at 1-800-767-1689 first.
  4. No hot water: check the breaker or pilot, and call if the unit leaks.
Before we arrive

What to do while the plumber is on the way

  • Move valuables and electronics off the floor.
  • Photograph the damage for your insurer.
  • Clear a path to the water heater, the main valve and the affected fixture.
  • Keep pets and children out of any room with sewage or standing water.
  • Have the address, gate code and a good callback number ready.

Tell the dispatcher your ZIP code, 84040 or 84041, and the nearest cross streets. It helps the plumber find you faster.

Layton local note

Layton's water and sewer, and what it means for a plumbing call

Layton is the largest city in Davis County, and its water comes from city wells, Weber Basin water and a secondary irrigation system. Culinary and irrigation water are separate, so keep indoor fixtures off the irrigation line. If you notice low pressure only in the yard, the irrigation side is the likely cause.

Sewage from Layton flows to the North Davis Sewer District, the same district that serves Syracuse. If a backup affects only your home, the blockage is on your side of the line. Layton City Public Works can be reached at 801.336.3720 for anything on the city side of the meter.
