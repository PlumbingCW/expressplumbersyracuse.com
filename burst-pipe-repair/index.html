Pipe burst: what to do in the first five minutes

Emergency burst pipe repair in Syracuse, UT is answered 24/7. Water damage grows every minute, so stop the flow first and call. These steps limit the damage while we are on the way.

  1. Shut the main water valve in the house, usually near the water heater or where the line enters.
  2. Turn off the water heater, and open a faucet at the lowest level to drain the lines.
  3. Switch off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel safely and dry.
  4. Move valuables, lift furniture legs onto blocks and start removing standing water.
  5. Call the line, and photograph the damage for your insurer.

If you cannot find the main shutoff, or it will not turn, do not touch the meter valve. Syracuse City Code says the city owns the shutoff valve at the meter, and city staff handle it. Call us, and we can also contact public works.

Frozen pipes

Frozen pipes before and after a split

A frozen pipe is a pipe waiting to burst. If a faucet gives only a trickle in a cold snap, the line may be frozen. Open the faucet, warm the pipe gently with a hair dryer on low and keep the cabinet doors open to warm air.

Never use an open flame, and never thaw a pipe you can see is split. A split pipe floods when it thaws, so shut the main first and call. After the thaw we repair the split section and look for other weak spots.

Local note

Frost protection is the owner's job

Syracuse City Code makes the owner responsible for the service lateral from the property side of the meter, and requires owners to protect it from frost. Failures in that stretch are the owner's repair and the owner's cost.

The same code lets plumbers shut off water at the curb box in a leak, and a permit from Public Works is needed to open or repair an existing lateral. Ask Public Works for the current rules before a dig, and call 811 first.

Prevention

Keeping pipes from freezing next winter

  • Disconnect garden hoses before the first hard freeze.
  • Insulate exposed pipes in garages, crawl spaces and exterior walls.
  • Open cabinet doors under sinks on cold nights.
  • Let a faucet drip when a hard freeze is forecast.
  • Keep the thermostat steady if you travel.
After the repair

Dry the damage and check your coverage

Once the water is stopped, dry the area quickly. Open windows, run fans and a dehumidifier, and lift wet carpet. Mold can start within a couple of days in damp materials.

Call your insurer and keep photos and receipts. We can describe the cause and the repair in writing for the claim.
