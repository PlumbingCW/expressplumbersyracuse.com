Emergency water heater replacement for a failed tank

Emergency water heater replacement in Syracuse, UT means a leaking or dead tank comes out and a new unit goes in, ideally on the same visit. Our line is answered 24/7 for floods under a heater, no hot water for a family, and tanks that have simply given up.

Close the cold supply valve above the heater, shut off the power at the breaker or the gas at the unit, and move anything stored near the base. If the tank is draining onto a finished floor, a wet-dry vacuum buys time until we arrive.

Before you decide

Signs the tank is finished

  • Water pooling around the base that does not come from a fitting.
  • Rust-colored hot water, which points to corrosion inside the tank.
  • A tank old enough that repairs keep stacking up.
  • Loud rumbling from sediment, with short hot water supply.
  • Moisture on the floor of a basement or garage corner that stays damp.

Not every cold heater needs replacing. If the tank is sound, a repair costs less, and we will say so.

Choosing the new unit

Tank, tankless, or hybrid

Standard tank

  • Lowest purchase price
  • Familiar parts and service
  • Standby heat loss
  • Hot water runs out under heavy use

Tankless or hybrid

  • Tankless gives continuous hot water.
  • Hybrid heat pump units use less electricity.
  • Higher purchase price and more install work.
  • Tankless needs a yearly flush.

Household size and hot water habits decide capacity. A family that runs a dishwasher, a washer and two showers at the same time needs a different unit than a couple. See tankless water heater service if continuous hot water is the goal.

The swap

What happens on replacement day

Confirm the setup

We check fuel type, venting, water and gas connections, and the space around the unit.

Drain and remove

The old tank is drained, disconnected and taken away.

Connect and vent

New unit set, supply and gas or electric connected, vent checked.

Test and review

Relief valve, discharge pipe, expansion needs and a hot water test before we go.

Gas units get a leak check at every connection, and we confirm the draft and venting suit the new unit. Replacing one tank with another is simple. Replacing a standard tank with a power vent or tankless unit is a bigger project, and the quote covers the extra venting or gas line work.

Local note

Newer homes, newer heaters, and what that means

Syracuse has grown from 3,702 residents in 1990 to roughly 40,000 today, so most homes here are fairly new. That usually means a water heater in a garage or utility closet with modern venting and copper or PEX supply lines, which makes a straight replacement more routine than in an older town.

It also means many heaters are now reaching the age where tanks fail. If your home was built in the early growth years and you have never replaced the heater, treat it as a planned job before it becomes an emergency.

What drives the price

What sets the cost of a replacement

  • Unit type and capacity: tank, hybrid or tankless.
  • Venting changes if the new unit needs a different vent than the old one.
  • Gas line, electrical or water connection upgrades.
  • Code items such as a discharge pipe, expansion tank or seismic strapping where required.
  • Hauling away the old tank and the hour of the call.

Permit rules vary by job, so ask the Syracuse City building department whether your swap needs one, and ask us about it too. You get a clear price before work starts.

Code note

What the rules mean for a swap

Utah's state construction code amends the International Plumbing Code (IPC) for use statewide, under Utah Code Title 15A, Chapter 3, Part 3. A like-for-like swap still involves a relief valve, a discharge pipe and correct venting, and those details are where a rushed job goes wrong.

Ask whether the quote includes a new relief valve, a code-compliant discharge pipe and an expansion tank where your pressure calls for one.
