Start with the symptom

Hidden leak? Here is how we find it

Emergency leak detection in Syracuse, UT is answered 24/7. If water is actively flowing, close the main shutoff while you wait, and if it is near electrical outlets or panels, stay out of the room. Then call and tell us what you see.

The work is locating water that is going where it should not, before anyone cuts into a wall or digs a trench. Most calls begin with a symptom: a meter that moves when everything is off, a bill that jumped, a stain, a warm spot, or the sound of water with no fixture running.

If water is flowing

Active leak right now: shut it off and call

  1. Find your main shutoff and turn it clockwise until it stops.
  2. If water is dripping near outlets, lights or the panel, turn off the breaker only if you can reach it dry.
  3. Move belongings out of the wet area and put a towel or bucket under drips.
  4. Call the 24/7 line. We will help decide whether this is a repair today or a scheduled detection visit.
Do it yourself

A ten-minute meter test for a hidden leak

You can confirm a leak before you call. Turn off every faucet, the ice maker and the irrigation, then look at the water meter. A moving dial or a spinning low-flow indicator means water is flowing somewhere.

  • Watch the leak indicator for a few minutes with everything off.
  • Close the main shutoff in the house. If the meter still moves, the leak is between the meter and the house.
  • If the meter stops, the leak is inside the house or in the irrigation line.
  • Note the reading and the time so we can compare a second reading later.
Where leaks hide

Ceiling, wall, floor and yard leaks

Ceiling or wall stain

Supply or drain lines inside the wall, or a fixture above. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging before opening anything.

Warm spot on the floor

A hot water line under a slab or a floor. This is the classic slab leak pattern.

Wet yard or low pressure

A leak on the line between the meter and the house, or on an irrigation line.

Damp crawlspace or under-house area

A drain or supply line leaking where you rarely look.

Slab leaks matter most in homes with concrete floors, including garages and additions. A basement home can still have a leak under a garage slab. Pool and spa lines get their own check when they are part of the property.

Local note

Your meter, your line, and your side of the leak

Syracuse City's water code says the city owns the shutoff valve and the water meter, and the property owner owns and maintains the service line from the property side of the meter. The meter sits in the park strip. A leak between the meter and your house is yours to fix, and work on an existing lateral requires a permit from Public Works.

Yard irrigation matters too. Syracuse sends pressurized secondary water to homes, and state law requires secondary meters by 2030. Once your irrigation is metered, a stuck sprinkler valve or a leaking line will show up in a way a flat fee never did. The city code also says to repair wasteful leaks, and the pressure irrigation chapter gives 15 days after notice.

Businesses and rentals

Commercial and property manager leaks

Landlords and shop owners call about leaks they cannot reach or cannot trace. We handle the detection and tell you whether the fix is a quick repair or a larger project. For restaurants and larger systems, the commercial plumbing page covers the rest of the work.

Pricing

What drives the cost of leak detection

  • How many methods are needed to find the leak.
  • Whether the leak is inside the house, under a slab or in the yard.
  • Access to the pipe, wall or crawlspace.
  • The time to confirm and document the leak for you or your insurer.
  • Repair is separate from detection, and we quote it once the leak is found.

Ask your insurer whether tracing a leak is covered. Many policies treat the water damage differently from the cost of finding the source.
