Tankless Water Heater Filter Systems in Syracuse, UT

Filters can protect a tankless heater, and the wrong filter on the wrong line wastes money. Here is what matters, and why a water test comes first.

By the Syracuse Express Plumbing team. Published November 16, 2026

First question

What problem are you solving?

People buy a filter for a tankless heater because they fear scale, sediment or both. Those are different problems, and a filter helps only one of them. A sediment filter catches grit before it reaches the heater. A softener or scale inhibitor addresses the dissolved minerals that form scale.

Without a test, you cannot say which one you need. Start there, and see tankless water heater service for how the heater itself works.

Compare

What each option does

OptionWhat it doesWhat it does not do
Sediment filterCatches sand, rust and gritDoes not remove dissolved hardness.
Scale inhibitorChanges how minerals form scaleDoes not soften the water.
Water softenerRemoves hardness mineralsAdds salt and needs a drain.
Carbon filterImproves taste and removes chlorineDoes not prevent scale.

Many homes combine a sediment filter with one of the scale options. A plumber can suggest a mix once the test is back.

Placement

Where the filter goes

  • On the cold water supply, before the heater.
  • Ahead of any branch that feeds the hose bibs, if you want to keep irrigation water off the filter.
  • With a bypass valve and shutoffs, so it can be serviced without a shutdown.
  • Accessible, with space to change cartridges.

A filter you cannot reach does not get serviced. A filter that is never serviced is worse than none, since it chokes the flow.

Local note

Two water systems, one filter line

Syracuse has culinary water for the house and a separate pressurized secondary system for irrigation. Filters and softeners belong on the culinary line only. Treating irrigation water wastes cartridges and salt, and connecting indoor fixtures to irrigation water is a hazard.

The state documents we found give no hardness figure for Syracuse, so test your tap and ask Syracuse City Public Works at 801.825.7235 what the supply looks like. A water softener is the right tool if the test shows high hardness.

Source type: City water documents and city code

Protect the warranty

Check the heater maker's water rules

Tankless manufacturers publish water quality limits, and some void the warranty if the water exceeds them. Read the manual, and ask the installer to confirm in writing which treatment the maker accepts.

If the heater has already started to scale, see water heater repair before adding a filter. A filter does not remove scale already inside the unit.

Step by step

How to decide, in order

Test the tap

Get hardness, iron and sediment readings.

Read the manual

Find the maker's water quality limits.

Pick the tool

Sediment filter for grit, softener for hardness.

Install and log

Place it on the cold supply and note the service dates.

Skipping the first step is the usual mistake. People buy a filter and still get scale, because the filter was never the right tool.

Maintenance

Keeping the filter from becoming the problem

  • Change cartridges on the maker's schedule or when flow drops.
  • Keep a spare cartridge on hand.
  • Open the bypass if you must work on the filter and the heater is running.
  • Watch for pressure drop at the shower, the first place it shows.

A filter that is never changed restricts flow, and a tankless heater needs a steady minimum flow to fire. Starve it and it can shut off in the middle of a shower.

Common questions

Three myths about tankless filters

Myth one: any filter stops scale. A sediment filter does not. Myth two: more filters are always better. Extra stages cut flow and raise the cost of service. Myth three: a filter replaces the yearly flush. It does not, because dissolved minerals pass straight through a sediment filter.

Ask the installer

Questions that expose a weak proposal

  • Which water test result is this filter based on?
  • Where will the bypass valve sit, and can I reach it?
  • How often do the cartridges change, and who sells them?
  • Does the heater maker accept this treatment for the warranty?

A proposal that cannot answer these is selling a box and not a solution. A good one names the test result, the placement and the service schedule up front.

Safety steps

What to do to work safely around a filter and a heater

  1. Turn off the gas and electricity to the heater before touching its water connections.
  2. Close the supply valve and relieve pressure before opening a filter housing.
  3. Do not let the heater fire with the water off. It can overheat and damage the unit.
  4. If you smell gas, leave the building, then call 911 and Enbridge Gas at 1-800-767-1689 first.
  5. Call a licensed plumber for any leak that does not stop when the valves are closed.
