Emergency water heater repair when the shower runs cold

Emergency water heater repair in Syracuse, UT starts with finding out why the tank is cold or leaking. A pilot, a thermostat, a heating element, a gas valve and a failed tank each have different fixes. The line is answered 24/7, nights and weekends included.

While you wait, check that the breaker has not tripped on an electric unit, and confirm the gas valve on a gas unit is open and nothing smells of gas. If water is pooling under the tank, close the cold supply valve above the heater and keep clear of the puddle.

Safety first

Gas smell or a flooding tank: what to do right now

If you smell gas, leave the building first. From a safe place, call 911 and the gas utility, Enbridge Gas, at 1-800-767-1689. Do not use light switches, phones inside, or anything that can spark, and do not try to turn gas valves yourself. Call us after the gas utility has cleared the house.

For a leaking tank with no gas smell, the steps are simpler.

  1. Turn off the power at the breaker, or set a gas unit to pilot or off using the control knob.
  2. Close the cold water supply valve on top of the heater.
  3. Open a hot tap upstairs to relieve pressure, then call the line.
Symptoms

What the symptoms usually mean

Pilot keeps going out

A weak thermocouple or a draft at the burner.

Lukewarm water

A failed heating element on an electric unit, or a dip tube and sediment problem on either type.

Popping or rumbling

Sediment cooked onto the tank bottom. A flush helps if the tank is otherwise sound.

Water at the base

A loose fitting is a repair. A seam leak in the tank is not, and means replacement.

Repair or replace

When a repair is worth it and when it is not

A new thermostat, element or gas control costs a fraction of a new tank, and it makes sense on a heater that is otherwise in good shape. A tank that leaks through the shell cannot be repaired. Corrosion has eaten through the steel, and the next failure is likely to be bigger.

Age is the tiebreaker. If a repair is more than a few years of life away from a replacement, we will say so before you spend the money. If the heater is a tankless unit, the better route is a flush or service on the tankless water heater page.

Local note

Syracuse's water and your heater

Syracuse's culinary water comes from a city well and from Weber Basin Water Conservancy District supply, according to the city's water plan on file with the state. The published plan does not list a hardness figure, so the honest answer on mineral buildup is to test your own tap.

That test matters for water heaters, because hard water leaves scale on elements and in the tank bottom. Syracuse City Public Works can point you to the annual water quality report, and a test kit tells you what is at your house.

What drives the price

What changes the cost of a water heater repair

  • The part: an igniter, thermostat or element is a smaller job than a gas control valve.
  • Fuel type and tank size, which decide parts and access.
  • How hard it is to reach the unit, for example a tight closet or an attic platform.
  • Whether a code issue, such as a missing discharge pipe, needs fixing while we are there.
  • An after-hours visit versus a scheduled one.

We quote the repair before starting. If the numbers show replacement is the smarter spend, we tell you so, and you decide.
