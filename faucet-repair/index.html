Faucet repair in Syracuse, UT

A dripping faucet wastes water all day, and a leak under the sink soaks the cabinet floor. Emergency faucet repair in Syracuse, UT is answered 24/7, and most fixes finish in a single visit. If water is spraying, close the valve under the sink first.

If the valve will not turn or water still flows, shut the main at the house. Do not touch the city meter valve. Syracuse City Code says only city agents adjust the meter, and a house shutoff is the one meant for you.

Types of faucet

Why different faucets fail differently

Cartridge and ceramic disc

  • Drip from the spout after shutoff.
  • Often a worn cartridge.
  • Replacing the cartridge restores the seal.

Compression and older washer types

  • Drip that worsens as you tighten.
  • Washers and seats wear out.
  • An old valve may need replacing.

We match parts to the make when we can. A faucet with no parts support is replaced, and we will tell you plainly when repair is not worth the cost.

Common calls

What we usually find

Drip from the spout

Worn cartridge, washer or seat.

Leak at the handle

Failing O-ring or cartridge seal.

Low flow

Mineral buildup in the aerator or a partly closed stop valve.

Wet cabinet

A loose supply line, a bad shutoff or a cracked fitting.

Hot and cold mix

Failing cartridge or a faulty check stop.

Low flow at one faucet usually points to the aerator. Low flow across the house is a different problem, and leak detection helps if you also see a high water bill.

Local note

Hard water and faucet life

Syracuse's culinary water comes from city wells and Weber Basin purchased water, and the state documents give no hardness number we can cite. If you see white crust on aerators and fixtures, that is a sign of mineral buildup, and a water test tells you how heavy it is. Syracuse City Public Works can say what the supply looks like at your address.

If buildup keeps returning, a water softener is worth a look, since it protects faucets, fixtures and the water heater together.

Outdoor

Hose bibs and frost

A hose bib left connected to a hose can freeze and split inside the wall. Syracuse City Code expects owners to protect service lines from frost, and the hose bib is the first fixture to feel it. Disconnect hoses before the first hard freeze, and shut off the interior valve to an outdoor spigot when the house has one.

If a spigot drips only when the hose is attached, the break is likely behind the wall. Call, and we will find the line before it turns into a flood. See burst pipe repair if water is already coming through.

Do not wait

Small leaks that grow

A drip under the sink wets the cabinet floor and swells the particleboard. Once it swells, the cabinet is a replacement and not a repair. If you see moisture, shut the stop valve and call before the damage spreads.
