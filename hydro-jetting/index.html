Overview

What hydro jetting does that a snake cannot

Our line is answered 24/7. If a main line is backing up into the house tonight, call now and stop using water until the plumber arrives. Emergency hydro jetting in Syracuse, UT sends high-pressure water through a hose and nozzle to scour the inside of a drain line.

A cable opens a channel through a clog. Jetting cleans the pipe wall, which is why it fits grease buildup, scale and root growth that keep coming back. It is not always the first tool, and we use a camera look to confirm the line can take it.

Active backup

Sewer backing up now: what to do first

  1. Stop running water: no laundry, no showers, no dishwasher.
  2. Do not flush toilets that are still draining.
  3. Keep people and pets out of any room with sewage on the floor.
  4. Call the 24/7 line and describe which drains are affected.

If sewage is coming up from the floor drain or lowest tub, the blockage is probably in the main line. We will clear it first, and then judge whether jetting, a camera look or a repair is the right follow up.

The process

What a jetting visit looks like

Find access

We locate a cleanout or pull a fixture to reach the line.

Camera look

A quick inspection shows the pipe condition and the blockage.

Jet the line

Water pressure and the right nozzle for the job, working through the line.

Verify

We rerun the camera or flow test to confirm the pipe is clear.

Jetting is loud and wet, so we protect floors and tell you where the water is going. We also point out when a plumbing change, such as adding a cleanout, would make the next service easier.

Local note

Who owns the line you are jetting

Syracuse homes connect to a sewer system operated by the North Davis Sewer District. The District's emergency page covers reports on its own lines, such as mains and manholes. Where your lateral ends and the District's line begins is worth confirming, and a camera run helps show it.

If a camera shows the blockage past your property, we will tell you, and you can report it to the District or to Syracuse City Public Works. The District lists daytime and after-hours lines on its emergency page.

Pricing

Why jetting costs what it does

  • Equipment: a jetter, hose reels and nozzles are heavy gear.
  • Line length and access, since a long run from a distant cleanout takes more time.
  • Condition of the pipe, which decides whether jetting is safe.
  • Whether a camera inspection is included before and after.
  • After-hours work versus a scheduled visit.

Ask for the quote to include the camera check. A jetting job without a look at the pipe can make a damaged line worse.
